# 2026-11-19 — suggested replies under the agent's message

The founder asked for options under the agent's message that continue the conversation, but that earn trust and lead to the person's goal and offline, never into an attention loop. The spec and mockups went for approval the same day (`docs/product/domains/suggested-replies.md`). The founder answered «Да, строй», which accepts the four recommended answers.

1. **Approved forks.**
   - Generation is a separate cheap call after the reply (`MODELS.fast`, strict JSON, 2.5 s cap, no retry, fails closed), not the agent's own call. The agent's output format and persona are untouched.
   - No «напомни в пятницу» step. The agent has no reminder tool, and a chip it cannot keep is a broken promise. Reminders, if wanted, are their own feature with a new table and a separate yes.
   - The exit «На сегодня всё» is answered by code: a line per time of day (day, evening, night) with variants, no model call, no question, no «пиши, если что».
   - In iOS the chips sit under the agent's latest bubble, not above the input field.
2. **Aligned with the agent concept** (`docs/product/domains/agent-concept.md`, session agent-audit). After «пока», «спасибо», «ок», «я спать» no chips at all, not even the exit (rule 6). A set is never a menu: either 2–3 answers to one question, or at most one step plus at most one clarify (rule 5). At night (23:00–05:00 local) only the exit plus at most one step.
3. **Steps are what the agent can do today.** `my_date` and `venue_change` with a booked date ahead, `date_feedback` after a date in the last three days without the person's feedback, `events` in the app only when `check_event` is in the turn's tools. `open_screen` has no calendar screen, so there is no calendar step.
4. **Safety.** The OpenAI moderation model runs on the person's turn in parallel with the chips call. Self-harm, violence, threats or a minor in it, or the chips model's own `safety` verdict, latch the chat: no chips until it ends (six quiet hours). Sexual talk alone does not latch, because frank talk is allowed by the stances.
5. **Dropped gate.** The spec's «profiler question is due» gate is not built: a profiler question is a row the server writes without a model, and its answer never runs an agent turn, so there is never a reply to put chips under.
6. **Contract.** `X-Gennety-Chat-Cards: replies`; `repliesPending` on `/v1/chat/message` and `/v1/chat/voice`; `GET /v1/chat/messages/{id}/replies` waits up to 3 s; `replies` on the newest history row; a tap is a turn with `suggestion: { messageId, id }`. Chips are stored in `messages.attachment` as kind `suggested_replies` and are never written over a card. No schema change.
7. **Telegram.** A one-time reply keyboard on the last bubble; the tap is recognised by the text matching the set kept in the bot session (`agentReplies`), and the exit gets the code-owned line with `remove_keyboard`. The chips call starts when the reply is ready and the typing pauses between bubbles cover it.
8. **Pulse.** Server-side metrics from the stored chips, match events (ACCEPTED, DATE_COMPLETED) and saved ideas: `agent_replies_shown_share`, `agent_replies_tap_rate{kind}`, `agent_replies_offline_step_7d` (the north star), `agent_replies_exit_share`, `agent_chat_turns_p50{arm}`, `agent_chat_offline_step_7d{arm}`, and the `replies_loop` insight. A stable 10% holdout by user-id hash gets no chips; preview-list people are never held out. Calendar slots and venue agreement have no timestamps and do not count as an offline step yet. The Telegram agent keeps its turns in `users.message_history`, so Telegram chips are counted in logs only (`[replies] telegram …`) until it moves onto message rows.
9. **Flag.** `AGENT_SUGGESTED_REPLIES_ENABLED` (default off) and `AGENT_SUGGESTED_REPLIES_USER_IDS` (preview, never held out). Off means no gate, no call, no field.
10. **Demo mode.** The same as production: the visitor talks to the real agent, and the chips show that the bot can be talked to. The exit answers the same way. Demo and synthetic accounts are excluded from Pulse by the existing filter.
11. **Measured on the live model** (`apps/bot/scripts/suggested-replies-eval.ts`, 16 scripted chats × 2, agent reply with the persona and the agent-concept block): 0 failures by code checks and a judge model; chips on 19% of replies, none on danger, self-harm, grief, a breakup or a goodbye; the long late chat got the exit alone. The chips call (model and moderation in parallel) took p50 1.3 s, max 2.3 s, with about 1.8k prompt and 35 completion tokens; the chips prompt ends with `CONCEPT_RULES`. An earlier run caught chips answering the agent's own «Хочешь, я посмотрю…?» — answers to the agent's offers are now forbidden.
12. **Founder, later the same day.**
   - The iOS look changes: chips look like ordinary message bubbles, but on the person's side (right-aligned, where their own messages are) and without the tail, stacked one under another. This replaces the glass capsules under the agent's bubble. Telegram keeps the reply keyboard, which is already on the person's side.
   - Reminders are approved as a full feature («напомни в пятницу» — «почему бы и нет, давай полноценно»). This reverses fork 2. They get their own plan for approval first, because they need a new table and a worker; the `remind` step chip joins this feature once reminders exist.
13. **Production.** The backend deploy waits for the founder's yes.
