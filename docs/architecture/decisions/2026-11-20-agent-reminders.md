# 2026-11-20 — personal reminders from the chat

On 2026-11-19, while suggested replies were being built, the founder reversed fork 2 of that spec: «напомни в пятницу — почему бы и нет, давай сделаем полноценно». The plan went to the founder as `docs/product/domains/agent-reminders.md`, and they answered all four open questions with the recommended option.

1. **Approved forks.**
   - The agent sets a reminder at once on the person's request, with a code-owned receipt («Напомню в пятницу, 10:00: написать Маше»). There is no confirmation card. Cancelling is a request in words.
   - The push shows the reminder text on the lock screen. The person wrote it for themselves, and iOS can hide previews.
   - The arriving reminder has two buttons, «Сделал» and «Напомни завтра». Snoozing is capped at three times. An ignored reminder never repeats.
   - Listing and cancelling happen in the chat only. No settings screen in this version.
2. **The rules that make it trustworthy.** Only on request, never set by the agent on its own. Only about life outside the chat; «come back and talk to me» is refused. The booked date's own reminders are not duplicated. Quiet hours hold unless the person named the exact time. The arriving message is code-owned, with no question and nothing after it (agent concept rules 6–7).
3. **Schema.** New table `agent_reminders`, a purely additive migration `20261120090000_agent_reminders`, generated by `prisma migrate diff`. The founder's «давай полноценно» plus the approved plan count as the yes for the schema change (AGENTS.md rule 5). It is not merged with `memory_reminders`: those belong to an idea and have no text of their own. A snooze moves `dueAt` and returns the row to `pending`, counted in `snoozes`; there is no separate `snoozed` state.
4. **Retention.** `done`, `cancelled`, and `sent` rows are deleted 30 days after their last change.
5. **Flag.** `AGENT_REMINDERS_ENABLED` (default off) and `AGENT_REMINDERS_USER_IDS` (preview). Off means no tools and no sends; rows that were set wait.
6. **Demo mode.** The same as production. The demo does not speed time up. Pulse excludes demo and synthetic accounts.
7. **Wording.** The button reads «Сделано», not «Сделал»: the founder's word is masculine in Russian, and the button is the same for everyone (uk «Зроблено», en «Done», de «Erledigt», pl «Zrobione»).
8. **The date in the prompt.** The prompts carry the weekday and the clock but never the date, and the first live run set «завтра вечером» two days off. Turns that offer the reminder tools now get a «Reminders» block with today's full date in the person's zone, plus «завтра» and «через неделю» as dates. After it: 0/24 failures in five languages (`apps/bot/scripts/reminders-eval.ts`).
9. **Production.** The deploy, with its migration, waits for the founder's yes.
